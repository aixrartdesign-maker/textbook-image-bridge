Feature · PRE-PRODUCTION MEETING

PPM,
the last agreement before the shoot

PPM stands for pre-production meeting. It is both the meeting just before shooting, where client · planning · direction · production lock every decision, and the document that records it. The approved PPM document becomes the reference for any later dispute. A verbal agreement that is not recorded does not exist. This feature walks through one PPM deck, page by page.

SPREAD 01 · Directing intentTone and manner board — the page that turns adjectives into measurements

"Warm and sophisticated" is not an agreement. A tone and manner board translates adjectives into measurable values of color temperature · contrast · saturation · texture · pace, so that camera · art · post look at the same numbers. Always review references together on screen. Describe a tone in words, and everyone imagines it differently.

TIP

Apply the palette equally to wardrobe · props · background · graphics. Send the swatch codes to the art and post teams in the same file.

In each reference panel, always write one word for "what you are referencing" (light · color · texture · movement · people). Copy a whole reference and it is plagiarism; take one element and it is study. The single anti-reference panel saves more meeting time than any other.

SPREAD 03 · CastingActor sheet — impression keywords and availability in the same panel

A casting page is not a row of headshots. First write the impression the concept demands as keywords, then put in the same panel how well each candidate fits them, along with schedule · fee · contract terms. What the client approves is not a face but "what this person will do for 15 seconds."

PhotosFour cuts under the same conditions

  • Neutral front · natural smile · profile · frame grab of movement
  • Unretouched, same lighting — the point is comparison
  • Selfies · glamour shots for reference only

TermsWrite these before the face

  • Available dates, and whether the backup day works
  • Fee unit (day/hour) and image-rights scope (media · term · region)
  • Wardrobe sizes, allergies, preferred angle, companions

Direction memoAgree in advance at the PPM

  • One line on 'what to tell this actor not to do'
  • Rehearsal time (+30 min if inexperienced)
  • If there is SYNC dialogue, attach a voice sample

SPREAD 05 · ScheduleShooting schedule — built around 'light and actors,' not dates

TIP

Group the schedule not by scene order but by location → light → actor availability. Shooting cuts with the same location · same lighting setup back to back saves 80% of the time.

  • Same setups back to back
  • 'Time you can't get back' first, like direct sun · golden hour
  • Actor call 45 min before the first cut

Safety margin+30% per cut, +1 hour per day

  • The first cut is always 30 min late
  • Travel: map time × 1.5
  • Lunch is a fixed 45 min; push it and the afternoon collapses

Backup dayIf it is not booked, it does not exist

  • Hold both actor · location
  • Set the rain threshold as a number (60% chance of rain)
  • Finalize the missed-cut list at the day's wrap-up

Always set aside a contingency

Without 10% of the total budget held in reserve, the project stops the moment rain, a late arrival, a gear failure, or a reshoot occurs. In a student project, securing one backup shooting day does the same job.

Meeting rules

  1. 01Send materials 48 hours before the meeting. No one can decide on material they first see in the room.
  2. 02Mark each item decided or on hold. Record an owner and a deadline with every hold.
  3. 03Always review references together on screen. Describe a tone in words, and everyone imagines it differently.
  4. 04Share the minutes the same day. By the next day, memories differ.
  5. 05There is one exit condition: every attendee must be able to say what they will do on shoot day.
  6. 06After the PPM, accept changes only through a change request. It states the cost · schedule impact and is signed by the approver.
Exercise2.2
Task
Make a mock PPM deck (twelve pages), split the teams into advertiser · production company roles, and run the meeting.
Tip
Include all of these: tone and manner board, location sheet, casting sheet, wardrobe · mise-en-scène map, schedule, call sheet, budget · risk tables. Don't forget the one anti-reference panel.
Assessment
Agreements recorded, questions handled, owner · deadline stated for items on hold
Model answer
Shares the minutes and the list of decisions within 1 hour of the meeting's end.

Task

Model answer
