Tools of light —
fixtures, beams, and light in software

Learning lighting is not memorizing fixture names; it is knowing which tool controls the size · distance · angle · intensity · color of light. This feature puts on one map the real fixtures and modifiers used on set, the beams from pin spot to ambient, and the After Effects · Premiere functions that rebuild the same light.

SPREAD 05Light in software — four After Effects 3D lights and light-effect tools

TIP

Software light 'adds to' the light on set; it does not 'make' it. Get the one shadow direction right during shooting, and you can layer anything on in post-production.

Names change (versions · plug-ins). What does not change is the order intensity → direction → size → color, and the rule that there is one shadow direction.

SPREAD 06On-set lighting cheat sheet — an order that gets setup done in 10 minutes

01OffTurn off every fluorescent · stray light in the room. Start from darkness.
02One keyKey at 45°/45° to the subject. Expose for the key (f/2.8, ISO 800 baseline).
03FillA reflector on the opposite side, or a soft at 1/3 intensity. This sets the contrast ratio.
04SeparateBack/rim or background light. Background 1 stop darker than the subject.
05Color · tidyUnify color temperature, cut spill with barn doors · flags, safety (sandbags · cables).

Contrast ratioKey : fill

  • 2:1 (1 stop) — bright, commercial, beauty
  • 4:1 (2 stops) — standard drama, interviews
  • 8:1 or more (3 stops+) — noir, thriller
  • No fill — silhouette, split

Color temperatureIf you mix, mean it

  • Daylight 5600K · tungsten 3200K · fluorescent 4000–4500K
  • Window + tungsten → CTO on the window or CTB on the lamp
  • Mix only one way: 'window=cool, interior=warm'
  • Set camera WB to the key light

SafetyAccidents start with lighting

  • A sandbag on every stand, cables taped to the floor
  • No gel · cloth within 30cm of tungsten (fire)
  • Add up the load: 1 household circuit ≈ 3kW
  • Above 2m, a 2-person job

Exercise4.7+
Task
Keep the same subject · same spot and shoot one cut in each of five lights: pin spot / spot / flood / soft / ambient. Draw a lighting plan (top view) for each cut. Recreate one of them in After Effects with Spot · Point · Ambient 3D lights.
Tip
No gear? Substitute a flashlight (pin spot), a desk lamp (spot), a window (soft), and an overcast day (ambient). What matters is seeing the difference in shadow edges with your own eyes.
Assessment
Difference in shadow edges across the five cuts, angles marked on the plans, matching shadow direction in the software recreation
Model answer
Place the five cuts side by side on one screen and caption each with one sentence on which emotion its light suits.
