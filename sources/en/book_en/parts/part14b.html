AppendixEquipment by budget
T

Equipment by budget

Gear isn't bought all at once; you add it in the order bottlenecks appear. This table helps you first make what you can with what you have, then decide what the next item should be.

Why the order matters

Upgrade the camera first and sound and light remain the problem, so results barely change. Get a mic and lights first, and even the same smartphone produces visibly better work.

The most common gear-buying mistake

Buying the most visible things first. Sound and light, which you can't see, decide quality first.

Five things every quote must state

Scope of work (number of pieces · length · versions), number of revisions, copyright ownership, usage rights (media · term · territory) and payment terms. A quote missing any of these five breeds disputes later. Also note whether the work may go in your portfolio.

PART 14 Wrap-up

Exercises and review

  1. Print and use the 6 worksheetsFill in the Appendix K templates as they are for a project in progress.
  2. Run through the checklists twiceOnce the day before the shoot and once just before delivery; note any item that gets flagged.

One-page summary

01 Trust paper

Don't trust memory; trust paper. Only what is written gets done.

02 Two checks

Run through the checklist once the day before the shoot and once just before delivery.

03 Worksheets that become a record

They are made to be printed and used. Fill them in for a semester and they become a record of your work.

04 What a checklist is for

It does not aid memory; it replaces memory. Don't try to memorize it; keep it open in front of you.

Questions to consider

What do I keep missing?
Can I turn it into a procedure? The moment a missed item becomes a line on a checklist, the mistake moves from a personal problem to a system problem.

Afterword

15 seconds is not short
