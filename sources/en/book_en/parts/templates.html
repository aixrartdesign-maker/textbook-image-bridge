8 storyboard templates

9-panel (or 8 · 6-panel) templates for each ratio. Copy them at A4, or enlarge to A3 and pin them to the wall. The five lines outside the panels are the same on every template — measurements, movement, action note, words, sound.

How to usePanel one is the legend

  • Ratio · resolution · version · date go in panel one
  • Arrows: black for people, wine for camera
  • Time: duration in seconds + running timecode

Vertical templatesAvoid UI overlays

  • No captions · logos inside the gray bands
  • Faces in the top 1/3, products in the center
  • 20–30% more cuts than horizontal

360 · curvedUnwrapped view + viewpoint map

  • Write the front angle in every panel
  • Keep total rotation under 180° per scene
  • Leave seams · floor · ceiling empty
